# Bumpus logo

`bumpus-logo.png`: a lean, short-coated bluetick coonhound with smooth
pendant ears, ticked coat, tan points and red-rimmed eyes, carrying a stolen
turkey drumstick above the "Bumpus" wordmark. 1254 × 1254 PNG with
transparency; the cream wordmark outline reads on light and dark
backgrounds. Embed at about 240 px:


Raster only: there is no editable vector master or separate avatar mark.

## Provenance

Generated 2026-10-07 with the built-in OpenAI image-generation tool from an
original-mascot logo prompt, then refined by edit prompts to the user's brief
(the Bumpus hounds of *A Christmas Story*: lean coonhounds, not bloodhounds
or long-haired dogs). No film stills or reference artwork were used and no
pixels were hand-edited. The final edit prompt:

> Redraw the dog completely as a lean short-coated bluetick coonhound, not a
> bloodhound or fluffy dog: narrow lean skull, long straight muzzle, small
> droopy dark eyes with muted red lower eyelid rims, hanging smooth jowls,
> very long thin flat smooth pendant ears, slim neck. Smooth short coat:
> dark charcoal/navy ears and crown, tan eyebrow and cheek spots, pale
> blue-gray muzzle and narrow chest with dark ticking. No fur tufts or
> feathered ears. Preserve the exact "Bumpus" typography, cream outline,
> turkey drumstick held sideways in the mouth and transparent background.
